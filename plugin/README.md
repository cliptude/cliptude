# Cliptude plugin for Claude

This plugin bundles the Cliptude connector (`https://mcp.cliptude.com/mcp`) with three skills: **cliptude** (make a video from a saved template), **cliptude-script** (research a topic and write the spoken narration) and **cliptude-plan** (direct every visual of the video yourself). Install it from the Claude directory, connect Cliptude on the plugin's Connectors tab and sign in with your Cliptude account. Videos use that account's credits; new jobs show their settings and credit price for you to confirm before they start.

**Data the plugin sends.** The skills run in Claude and send nothing themselves. The connector sends Cliptude what you ask it to work with: video titles, scripts or prompts, template settings, product page URLs, and any audio, video or images you upload. Cliptude stores these with your account to produce and keep your videos. Sign-in uses OAuth on cliptude.com; the plugin holds no API keys.

**Other services the skills may use.** When you ask Claude to find the media for a plan itself (a render-only plan in **cliptude-plan**), the skill suggests searching free stock and public-domain sources with Claude's own tools: Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia Commons, NASA, the Library of Congress, the US National Archives and the Internet Archive, plus stock sites you subscribe to (Storyblocks, Vecteezy, Shutterstock, Getty Images, Adobe Stock, Envato, Pond5, Unsplash) and download services such as VidKraken and Oxylabs. Those searches send your search terms to that service, and any key used is your own, kept in your agent's environment, never sent to Cliptude. **cliptude-script** researches facts with Claude's web search. Files you upload go straight to Cliptude's storage through a signed link. See the [privacy policy](https://cliptude.com/privacy/) and [terms](https://cliptude.com/terms/).

The CLI and the self-hosted MCP server live in the [repository root](https://github.com/cliptude/cliptude).
